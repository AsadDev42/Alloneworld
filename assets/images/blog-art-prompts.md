# Blog artwork

Created with the built-in image_gen tool for the homepage demo. All three selected outputs are saved beside this file. These are editorial illustrations, not documentary photographs.

## blog-art-belonging.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single square editorial art image for the All One World psychosocial community blog, topic Attachment, Loss and Belonging. Contemporary painterly mixed-media illustration: two abstract human silhouettes on separate warm terracotta islands, linked by one delicate golden thread that arcs gently over a calm deep-teal sea. Spacious, thoughtful, humane, sophisticated hand-painted paper texture, soft dusk light, subtle geometric architectural echoes. Teal, warm sunset ochre, sandstone, muted coral, off-white. Strong simple composition legible in a square website card. Full bleed, no text, no lettering, no logos, no borders. Not a photo and not a UI mockup.

## blog-art-identity.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single square premium editorial mixed-media illustration for a psychosocial community blog, topic Identity, Difference and Relationships. Layered translucent human profile silhouettes cut from textured painted paper, each a different muted teal, clay, ochre, and cream, overlapping into a harmonious abstract composition. Subtle fragments of geometric lattice and organic botanical curves connect the profiles. Reflective and welcoming, sophisticated printmaking texture and painterly shading, quiet modern art direction, balanced and readable in a large square website card. Full bleed, no text, no letters, no logos, no borders. Not a photo or UI mockup.

## blog-art-healing.png

Warm natural dusk light, painterly gouache and textured handmade paper, deep teal, terracotta, pale sand, muted sage and golden ochre. Contemporary, dignified, reflective and grounded, no medical promises or religious text. Strong uncluttered composition readable as a square blog card. Full bleed artwork, no text, no letters, no logos, no borders, not a photo or UI mockup.
